    Long bio

    DJ PH is a South African DJ, broadcaster, recording artist, executive producer, television producer, podcaster and entrepreneur. His career spans nearly two decades across music, media, entertainment and business, and it started in 2009, straight out of school, chasing the feeling of a six to six at Bonza Bay in East London.

    Behind the decks he is known for dynamic sets that blend hip hop, house and R&B, and for a stage presence that holds a room. He has played major events and festivals in South Africa and abroad, including SXSW in Austin, Texas. In December 2019 he and Shimza went back to back for seventy-two hours at Icon in Soweto, a world record, and his single "Go Down", featuring Manu Worldstar and Rouge, was the official soundtrack of the 2019 Africa Cup of Nations.

    On radio he has hosted Cruise Control on Metro FM and Party with PH on 5FM, where he is back every Friday from 18:00 to 22:00. The Shady PHodcast takes that conversation further. On television he was a VJ on SABC 1's Live AMP and executive produced Lockdown House Party.

    Off air he works across talent, entertainment and commercial partnerships, so he sees every project from the creative and the strategic side. The biggest expression of that is PHola. What began in 2017 as a private birthday celebration is now a premium daytime festival with editions in Johannesburg, Cape Town, Durban, East London, Bloemfontein and Botswana, built on quality production, cultural relevance and a room where everyone feels welcome.

    Across music, radio, television, podcasting and live experiences, PH has built a platform that genuinely connects with people, which makes him a natural partner for brands that want authentic engagement in music, lifestyle and entertainment.

    About PH

    God first. Then the people on the floor.

    DJ, broadcaster, recording artist, executive producer, television producer, podcaster and entrepreneur. Born in the Eastern Cape, based in Johannesburg, and rarely in one place for long.
